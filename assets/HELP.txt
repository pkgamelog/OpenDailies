=================================================
 OpenDailies - Quick Help & Shortcuts
=================================================

1. PLAYBACK & TIMELINE
   - Spacebar: Play / Pause
   - Right Arrow: Next Frame
   - Left Arrow: Previous Frame
   - Comma (,): Jump to Previous Annotation
   - Period (.): Jump to Next Annotation

2. ANNOTATIONS (Left Toolbar)
   - pen: Standard smooth brush.
   - Pencil: Textured brush (responds to Pen Tablet pressure).
   - Shapes: Click and drag to draw Rectangles, Arrows, and Circles.
   - Text: Click to place a text box.
   - Eraser: Click on a stroke to delete it.
   - Undo: Reverts the last action on the current frame.
   - Clear Frame: Deletes all annotations on the current frame.

3. ONION SKINNING
   - Click the overlapping circles icon.
   - Set "Before" (past frames) and "After" (future frames) to see them tinted Blue and Orange.
   - Adjust Opacity to fade the onion skin.

4. COMMENTS & BOOKMARKS (Right Panel)
   - Comments are tied to the exact frame you are on. Double-click a comment to jump back to that frame.
   - Bookmarks mark specific frames. They appear as colored triangles on the timeline.

5. EXPORTING (File Menu)
   - Export Trim: Quickly cuts the video using the Blue In/Out handles on the timeline.
   - Export Playblast: Burns annotations, timecode, and subtitles directly into the video.
   - Export SRT: Generates a subtitle file from your bookmarks.
   - Export JSON: Exports all review data for pipeline integration.

=================================================
 For more tutorials and updates, visit:
https://discord.gg/y4AdT3s87

   - Text: Click to place a text box.
   - Eraser: Click on a stroke to delete it.
   - Undo: Reverts the last action on the current frame.
   - Clear Frame: Deletes all annotations on the current frame.

3. ONION SKINNING
   - Click the overlapping circles icon.
   - Set "Before" (past frames) and "After" (future frames) to see them tinted Blue and Orange.
   - Adjust Opacity to fade the onion skin.

4. COMMENTS & BOOKMARKS (Right Panel)
   - Comments are tied to the exact frame you are on. Double-click a comment to jump back to that frame.
   - Bookmarks mark specific frames. They appear as colored triangles on the timeline.

5. EXPORTING (File Menu)
   - Export Trim: Quickly cuts the video using the Blue In/Out handles on the timeline.
   - Export Playblast: Burns annotations, timecode, and subtitles directly into the video.
   - Export SRT: Generates a subtitle file from your bookmarks.
   - Export JSON: Exports all review data for pipeline integration.
